EDUShasta — a free browser for school and learning

EDUShasta (Shasta for short) is a free, education-friendly web browser that runs in the browser. Students can research, study, read documents and run classroom learning web apps from any computer, including school Chromebooks, with tabs, bookmarks and an on-device study assistant.

For education

  • Open learning websites and study tools
  • Read and annotate PDFs for homework and research
  • A built-in app store of classroom apps and games
  • A private study assistant that runs on the device

Used for learning at school

Shasta is where students open classroom websites and study tools, and it links straight to the resources they already use: Khan Academy, Wikipedia, Desmos, Scratch, BrainPOP and Coolmath.

Questions

Is EDUShasta free?

Yes. Everything that does something is free, with no account to create. Supporters can turn off adverts.

Does it work on a school Chromebook?

Yes. EDUShasta runs in a normal Chrome tab, so there is nothing to install. On Chromebooks that allow it, it can also be installed as an app.

What is the study assistant?

Shasta AI summarizes pages, explains a selection, makes quizzes and flashcards, and answers questions about your notes and PDFs. It runs on your own device, so your files and recordings are not sent to a server.
